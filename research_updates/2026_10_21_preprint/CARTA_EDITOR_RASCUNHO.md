Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Nonexistence of homogeneous rotation symmetric bent functions of degree four in twelve variables” for publication in Designs, Codes and Cryptography.

The manuscript proves nonexistence for the complete quartic rotation symmetric space in twelve variables, comprising all 2^43 choices of coefficients in its 43-orbit basis. The proof combines necessary conditions obtained from restricted weight divisibility and periodic autocorrelations with finite affine refutations certified in Lean 4.35.0. The exposition provides the reduction and soundness arguments; the accompanying material supplies the concrete certificates and verification records.

To make review practical, a compact independent Python checker verifies an affine-decomposition certificate and all terminal Walsh witnesses without repeating the original exhaustive search. The complete mathematical Lean source archive is supplied separately, with source hashes matched to the certified records. The manuscript clearly distinguishes that independent replay from a full fresh Lean rebuild.

A related preprint by the author, “Antipodal Necessity and the Cubic Case of the Homogeneous Rotation-Symmetric Bent Function Conjecture” (https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34165260), treats a structural antipodal theorem and the cubic case. The present manuscript has a different main theorem and proof method and does not depend on that result as a hypothesis. Its relationship to any concurrent journal submission will be disclosed with the applicable submission identifier.
