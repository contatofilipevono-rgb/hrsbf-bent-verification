# Post-2015 literature audit: antipodal necessity and cubic HRSBF

Date: 2026-10-06

## Questions

1. Is there a published theorem stating that every rotation-symmetric bent Boolean function of degree <=3 in N=2^k variables must contain the antipodal quadratic orbit P_N?
2. Is there a published theorem excluding homogeneous cubic rotation-symmetric bent functions in every even dimension?

## Closest antecedent: Cusick–Sanger 2017

Cusick and Sanger prove that for n=2m every quadratic rotation-symmetric bent function must contain

    P_n = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} x_i x_{m+i}.

They also prove an antipodal-necessity result for sums composed of short-cycle MRS functions, and obtain substantial cases of the homogeneous conjecture for n=2p.

This is the closest structural antecedent located. It does not cover arbitrary degree<=3 RS functions containing long cubic orbits, which is the domain of the manuscript's power-of-two Antipodal Rule.

## Wang–Liu–Zhao–Lin 2015

Their three cubic RS bent constructions in n=2^(k+3) variables all explicitly contain P_n. Their full paper continues to state the homogeneous degree>2 nonexistence statement as a conjecture. Thus the constructions are strong positive controls for the Antipodal Rule but not a universal necessity theorem.

## Short k-rotation literature (2024)

The 2024 paper "Short k-rotation symmetric Boolean functions" characterizes short k-cycles. Its public text still describes the nonexistence of homogeneous rotation-symmetric bent functions of degree >2 as an open conjecture.

## Sun–Shi–Liu–Fu 2026

The 2026 Designs, Codes and Cryptography paper states in its abstract that it obtains new nonexistence results that solve the conjecture partially. Our prior full-text audit likewise found conditional/arithmetic cases rather than a uniform cubic theorem for every even n.

## Other recent construction literature

Generic/construction papers in 2021 and 2024 build rotation-symmetric bent functions of various degrees. A 2026 work studies RS bent functions outside the completed Maiorana–McFarland class and computationally classifies cubic RS bent functions in ten variables. These results concern construction/equivalence and do not supply the universal antipodal necessity or global homogeneous cubic nonexistence statement targeted here.

## Current literature conclusion

After searches by title, conjecture terminology, degree, power-of-two dimension, short-cycle MRS terminology, n/2-shift terminology, and the explicit antipodal quadratic form, no prior result was identified that states either:

- the manuscript's Antipodal Rule for all degree<=3 RS bent functions in power-of-two dimension; or
- the manuscript's homogeneous cubic nonexistence theorem uniformly for every positive even dimension.

This remains a literature-qualified conclusion, not an absolute priority proof.

## Recommended positioning

Present the Antipodal Rule as an extension in scope of the antipodal necessity known in the quadratic/short-cycle setting (Cusick–Sanger), and cite Wang et al. as a power-of-two cubic positive control. Avoid claiming novelty for the quadratic antipodal necessity itself.
