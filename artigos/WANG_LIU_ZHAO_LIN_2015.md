# Wang–Liu–Zhao–Lin (ISIT 2015) — comparison with the Antipodal Rule

## Reference

T. Wang, M. Liu, S. Zhao, D. Lin, "Construction of Cubic Rotation Symmetric Bent Functions in Power-of-Two Variables", 2015 IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT), pp. 486–490. DOI: 10.1109/ISIT.2015.7282502.

The full paper was obtained and audited during the project.

## What the paper proves

The authors construct three cubic rotation-symmetric bent functions in n=2^(k+3), k>=0, variables, solving the construction problem left open by Gao et al. They prove bentness by an invertible affine transformation to Maiorana–McFarland form.

The introduction explicitly distinguishes these nonhomogeneous constructions from the conjecture that no homogeneous rotation-symmetric bent function of degree greater than two exists.

## Direct comparison with the Antipodal Rule

For n=8*2^k, the antipodal displacement is n/2=4*2^k. Every one of the three displayed constructions (equations (2), (3), and (4)) contains the quadratic term

    sum_{i=0}^{4*2^k-1} x_i x_{4*2^k+i},

which is precisely the antipodal quadratic orbit P_n.

Thus all three published cubic RS bent families in these power-of-two dimensions satisfy the structural necessity predicted by the manuscript's Antipodal Rule.

This is a strong external positive control, but it is not a proof of the universal rule: Wang et al. construct three families; they do not classify all degree<=3 RS bent functions.

## Novelty/overlap conclusion

No theorem in this paper states that every degree<=3 rotation-symmetric bent function in power-of-two dimension must contain P_n. The paper continues to cite the homogeneous degree>2 nonexistence statement as a conjecture. Therefore it does not subsume the manuscript's global homogeneous cubic nonexistence theorem.
