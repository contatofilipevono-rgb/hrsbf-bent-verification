# Exterior-square certificate for the quartic base block

Let V=F_2^8 and let f_4 be the homogeneous quartic part of the certified RS bent base function, with orbit seeds (0,1,2,3), (0,1,2,5), (0,1,3,5). Let

    T(a,b,c,d)=D_aD_bD_cD_d f_4.

It induces

    Phi: Lambda^2 V -> (Lambda^2 V)^*

by

    Phi(a wedge b)(c wedge d)=T(a,b,c,d).

In characteristic two, T is alternating in the sense needed to descend to exterior squares: D_aD_a g is identically zero for every Boolean function g, so T vanishes whenever two arguments coincide.

## Exact computation

In the standard 28-dimensional basis,

    rank(Phi)=22,
    dim ker(Phi)=6.

Thus the stronger claim that Phi is injective is false.

The relevant condition is weaker: ker(Phi) must contain no nonzero decomposable 2-vector. Its 63 nonzero elements, viewed as alternating 8x8 matrices, have exact rank spectrum

    60 of rank 8,
    3 of rank 4,
    0 of rank 2.

A nonzero decomposable 2-vector a wedge b has alternating-matrix rank 2, over F_2 as over any field. Hence ker(Phi) contains no nonzero decomposable 2-vector.

## Consequence for relaxed M-subspaces

Assume U<=V is a relaxed M-subspace with dim U>=2. Choose linearly independent a,b in U. By definition,

    D_aD_b f

is constant. Therefore, for every c,d in V,

    D_cD_dD_aD_b f = 0.

All terms of degree <4 disappear after four derivatives, so

    T(a,b,c,d)=0

for all c,d. Equivalently,

    Phi(a wedge b)=0.

Since a,b are independent,

    a wedge b != 0,

and it is decomposable. This contradicts the absence of nonzero rank-2 elements in ker(Phi). Therefore no relaxed M-subspace can have dimension at least 2, and

    r-ind(f) <= 1.

For completeness, every nonzero a in V gives a one-dimensional relaxed M-subspace <a>, because

    D_aD_a f = 0.

Hence

    r-ind(f) >= 1,

and therefore

    r-ind(f)=1.

For a two-dimensional subspace U=<a,b>, it is also useful to note explicitly that

    D_aD_{a+b} f = D_aD_b f + D_aD_a f = D_aD_b f,

so controlling one independent pair controls the other nontrivial pairings generated by the same basis. Any relaxed M-subspace of dimension at least 3 contains a two-dimensional subspace, so the independent-pair obstruction is sufficient.

Since ordinary M-subspaces are in particular relaxed M-subspaces,

    ind(f)=1.

## General criterion

For any quartic Boolean function f, if the kernel of its fourth-polarization map

    Phi_f: Lambda^2 V -> (Lambda^2 V)^*

contains no nonzero decomposable 2-vector, then

    r-ind(f)=1.

The proof is exactly the argument above: a relaxed M-subspace of dimension at least two supplies independent a,b with constant D_aD_b f, hence a nonzero decomposable a wedge b in ker(Phi_f); conversely every one-dimensional subspace is relaxed because D_aD_a f=0.

## Scope

This replaces the previous exhaustive check of 32,385 independent pairs at 256 points each by one 28x28 polarization matrix and a structural analysis of its kernel. No novelty claim is made here without a dedicated priority review.
