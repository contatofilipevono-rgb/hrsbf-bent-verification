# Kernel structure of the quartic polarization

For the certified n=8 quartic RS bent base block, let K=ker(Phi_f) inside Lambda^2(F_2^8). The exact exterior-square computation gives dim K=6 and nonzero alternating-rank spectrum 3 elements of rank 4 and 60 elements of rank 8.

A further exact check shows that the three rank-4 elements are closed under pairwise addition: if they are u,v,w, then u+v=w. Hence they are precisely the three nonzero elements of a distinguished 2-dimensional subspace R<=K.

Therefore K has the sharp internal decomposition by rank:
- R\{0}: exactly 3 elements, all alternating rank 4;
- K\R: exactly 60 elements, all alternating rank 8.

In particular K has no nonzero rank-2 element and therefore no nonzero decomposable 2-vector. This recovers the exterior-square obstruction r-ind(f)=1.

This strengthens the earlier certificate: the exceptional rank-4 locus is not an accidental set of three points but a linear plane over F_2. The next structural question is whether R is characterized intrinsically by the C_8 rotation action on K, which could give a symbolic explanation of the rank spectrum.

Literature note: constant-rank and bounded-rank spaces of alternating matrices are classical. This note claims only the exact structure computed for this kernel, not novelty of the ambient matrix-space theory.
