# V2 — 2026 literature correction: ell-optimal bent functions

Date: 2026-10-07

## Why this note is necessary

A broader priority search located 2026 work by Kudin, Pasalic, Polujan, Zhang and Zhao on permutations satisfying (P1)/(P2) and ell-optimal bent functions.

The paper explicitly introduces ell-optimal bent functions as bent functions attaining the lowest possible linearity index. It gives explicit constructions in the D_0 class and uses them in secondary constructions outside the completed Maiorana–McFarland class.

Therefore the manuscript must NOT suggest that:
- linearity index 1 is new in itself;
- constructing bent functions with minimal possible linearity index is new;
- prescribed/small linearity index is unexplored.

## What remains distinct in our project

Our theorem concerns a specific infinite quartic rotation-symmetric family F_r on n=8r variables with

    ind(F_r) = r-ind(F_r) = r = n/8.

The construction is an interleaved coordinate permutation of an r-fold direct sum of a certified 8-variable quartic RS bent block F1 with

    ind(F1) = r-ind(F1) = 1.

The 2026 ell-optimal work therefore changes the novelty framing but does not, from the material located in this audit, reproduce the exact all-r identity n/8 for this quartic RS family.

## Updated defensible wording

> We determine the exact linearity index of an explicit infinite quartic rotation-symmetric bent family in dimensions n=8r, obtaining ind(F_r)=n/8. Recent work has developed explicit bent functions with minimal or prescribed small linearity index in other construction classes; accordingly, our contribution is the exact quantitative index theorem for this rotation-symmetric quartic family, not the general concept of low-linearity-index bent functions.
