# V2 — Complete M-subspace spectrum

Date: 2026-10-07

## Main structural theorem

Let f:F_2^8 -> F_2 be the quartic RS bent seed with certified

    r-ind(f)=1,

and let F_r be its r-fold direct sum (equivalently, the interleaved RS realization) on n=8r variables.

For every k with 1 <= k <= r,

    RMS_k(F_r) = MS_k(F_r).

More precisely, a k-dimensional subspace U is an M-subspace of F_r if and only if every block projection p_j(U) has dimension at most one.

### Proof

If U is relaxed, iterating the projection argument of Polujan--Pott Theorem 4.5 shows p_j(U) is a relaxed M-subspace of f. Since r-ind(f)=1,

    dim p_j(U) <= 1

for every block j.

Conversely, suppose every p_j(U) has dimension <=1. For each nonzero projection choose its unique line L_j=<a_j>. For any u,v in U, their jth components are alpha_j a_j and beta_j a_j. Hence the block second derivative is zero: one direction is zero, or both equal a_j and D_{a_j}D_{a_j}f=0. Summing over disjoint blocks gives D_uD_vF_r=0. Thus U is an ordinary M-subspace.

Since MS is always contained in RMS, equality follows for every dimension.

## Support decomposition

For an M-subspace U define its block support

    S(U)={j : p_j(U) != 0}.

Let s=|S(U)|. Then k <= s <= r.

For every j in S(U), the image p_j(U) is a uniquely determined one-dimensional line L_j in F_2^8. There are

    2^8-1 = 255

choices for each line.

After choosing the s active lines, their product is canonically isomorphic to F_2^s. The condition that S(U) is exactly the active support means that the corresponding k-dimensional subspace of F_2^s has nonzero projection on every coordinate, equivalently it is not contained in any coordinate hyperplane.

## Full-support Gaussian count

Let A(s,k) denote the number of k-dimensional subspaces of F_2^s with full coordinate support.

By inclusion-exclusion over the s coordinate hyperplanes,

    A(s,k)
      = sum_{j=0}^s (-1)^j * C(s,j) * [s-j choose k]_2,

where [m choose k]_2 is the Gaussian binomial coefficient

    [m choose k]_2
      = product_{i=0}^{k-1} (2^{m-i}-1)/(2^{k-i}-1),

with the convention [m choose k]_2=0 for m<k.

## Complete counting formula

Therefore

    |MS_k(F_r)| = |RMS_k(F_r)|
      = sum_{s=k}^r C(r,s) * 255^s * A(s,k)

and hence

    |MS_k(F_r)| = |RMS_k(F_r)|
      = sum_{s=k}^r C(r,s) 255^s
          sum_{j=0}^s (-1)^j C(s,j) [s-j choose k]_2.

This gives the complete M-subspace spectrum for every dimension 1<=k<=r.

## Sanity checks

### k=1

A(s,1)=1: over F_2 the only full-support line in F_2^s is the line generated by (1,...,1).

Thus

    |MS_1(F_r)|
      = sum_{s=1}^r C(r,s)255^s
      = 256^r-1
      = 2^{8r}-1,

which is correct because every one-dimensional subspace of F_2^{8r} is automatically an M-subspace.

### k=r

Only s=r contributes and A(r,r)=1, giving

    |MS_r(F_r)|=255^r,

recovering the previous maximal-subspace theorem.

### k>r

There are no relaxed M-subspaces, hence no M-subspaces:

    MS_k(F_r)=RMS_k(F_r)=empty.

## Structural interpretation

The entire M-subspace geometry of F_r is determined by:
1. choosing a subset S of active 8-variable blocks;
2. choosing one of 255 lines in each active block;
3. choosing a full-support binary k-subspace inside F_2^{|S|}.

Thus the seed property r-ind(f)=1 propagates to a complete combinatorial classification of all M-subspaces of the infinite family.

## Novelty positioning

Polujan--Pott provides the general projection/containment theorem for relaxed M-subspaces of direct sums. The specialization r-ind(f)=1 is what collapses the geometry enough to obtain the explicit spectrum above.

The formula should be presented as a family-specific corollary/classification derived from the certified seed plus known direct-sum theory, not as a new general direct-sum theorem.

A dedicated literature search is still required before claiming priority for the complete spectrum formula.
