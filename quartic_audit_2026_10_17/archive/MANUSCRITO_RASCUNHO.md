# Sharp local-autocorrelation trade-offs and finite spectral certificates for homogeneous rotation-symmetric quartics

Research draft, 17 October 2026. Bibliographic originality and venue suitability remain to be established. This is a working manuscript, not a claim of a solved general conjecture.

## Abstract

We study homogeneous quartic Boolean functions invariant under cyclic rotation. For eight and ten variables, we determine the complete Pareto frontier between nonlinearity and the sum of squared autocorrelations in directions of Hamming weight at most two. The ten-variable frontier consists of four points, and the functions maximizing nonlinearity do not minimize this local energy. We also determine the minimum size of a fixed set of Walsh frequencies certifying the sharp nonlinearity bound: it is two for eight variables and seventeen for ten variables. The latter lower bound is supported by an independently checked unsatisfiability certificate. In sixteen variables, orbit-code weight congruences prove three linear conditions and a factored quadratic condition necessary for bentness, yielding an explicit candidate parametrization by 112 free bits. A factored six-condition normal form is controlled by eleven aggregate parities and leaves exactly 3/128 of the original coefficient space. Combining three additional periodic second bits through one vector-character lemma gives a rigorous pass fraction at most 2097035386883/562949953421312, excluding at least 99.6274916848046%. All coefficient models and 4,800 quadratic sums have independent checks. We describe a coefficient-only quintic obstruction to PC(2) and report clearly delimited searches. The nonexistence of homogeneous rotation-symmetric quartic bent functions in sixteen variables is not established.

## 1. Class, conventions, and context

Let H(n,4) be the F₂-vector space generated by the cyclic orbits of squarefree monomials of degree four. An orbit generator sums its distinct monomials once. This convention is essential for short orbits. We include the zero function in the enumerated vector space.

For f:F₂ⁿ→F₂, let W_f(s)=Σ_x(−1)^{f(x)+s·x}, C_f(a)=Σ_x(−1)^{f(x)+f(x+a)}, and NL(f)=2^{n−1}−½max_s|W_f(s)|. Define

Q₂(f)=Σ_{1≤wt(a)≤2} C_f(a)²,

and E₂(f)=C_f(e₀)²+Σ_{d=1}^{n/2}C_f(e₀+e_d)². For even n, cyclic symmetry gives

Q₂=n[C_f(e₀)²+Σ_{d=1}^{n/2−1}C_f(e₀+e_d)²]+(n/2)C_f(e₀+e_{n/2})².

These energies have different multiplicities. PC(2) is equivalent to Q₂=E₂=0. Bentness implies zero autocorrelation in every nonzero direction and therefore implies PC(2).

The conjectured absence of homogeneous rotation-symmetric bent functions of degree greater than two predates this work [1]. Recent partial nonexistence results also use derivatives and nonlinearity [2]. Our proposed focus is the exact trade-off and certificate complexity in explicitly finite classes, together with the sixteen-variable coefficient obstruction. The full recent literature must be checked before any novelty statement is made.

## 2. Complete finite Pareto frontiers

**Computational Theorem 1.** Among all functions in H(8,4), the nondominated (NL,Q₂) pairs, maximizing the first coordinate and minimizing the second, are

(110,50176), (92,47104), (88,40960),

with respectively 2, 4, and 4 functions attaining these pairs. Among all functions in H(10,4), the frontier is

(480,92160), (476,66560), (472,53760), (468,38400),

with respectively 4, 4, 8, and 4 functions attaining these pairs.

The coefficient spaces have dimensions ten and twenty-two, so the enumerations contain 1,024 and 4,194,304 functions. Counts do not quotient by equivalence. There are 116 ten-variable functions with maximum nonlinearity 480, but only four attain the smallest Q₂ within this stratum. Their Q₂ is 92,160, whereas the global Q₂ minimum is 38,400 at nonlinearity 468.

Under variable permutations x_i↦x_(ki mod n) with gcd(k,n)=1, the ten-variable equality cases form respectively one, one, two, and one orbits at the four frontier points. The 116 maximum-nonlinearity functions form 29 orbits under this group. These are classifications under a specified normalizer subgroup, not under all affine equivalences.

### Computation and independent verification

Write f_c=Σ_j c_jG_j. For any frequency s, W_{f_c}(s) is the Walsh transform, in coefficient space, of the signed multiplicities of the signatures (G_j(x))_j. Autocorrelations are obtained from derivative signatures (G_j(x)+G_j(x+a))_j. Integer transforms yield all values for all coefficient words simultaneously.

A second enumeration uses Gray order, updates the packed truth table by XOR with one generator, counts autocorrelations directly, and computes Walsh coefficients as distances to linear-character truth tables. The two methods agree on the individual NL, Q₂, and maximum low-weight autocorrelation for every function in both spaces. Parseval was additionally checked for every coefficient word in the transform-based computation.

## 3. Minimum fixed spectral test sets

For a threshold T, call S a certificate set if every f in the class has |W_f(s)|≥T for some s∈S. Such a set certifies NL(f)≤2^{n−1}−T/2. The set is fixed independently of f.

**Computational Theorem 2.** In H(8,4), the minimum certificate-set size for T=36 is two. The set {0,e₀} is optimal. In H(10,4), the minimum certificate-set size for T=64 is seventeen. Using integer masks to label frequencies, one optimal set is

{0,1,3,5,9,17,33,165,189,207,223,231,251,351,375,495,1023}.

For the upper bound, its union covers all 4,194,304 coefficient words. For the lower bound, 1,175 witness functions give clauses requiring a selected frequency from each witness's qualifying set. These clauses together with an at-most-sixteen cardinality constraint are unsatisfiable. A DRUP certificate was generated; all 4,480 added clauses were verified by reverse unit propagation using a separate solver, including the final empty clause. All qualifying frequencies of the witnesses were recomputed directly from their ANFs.

Cyclic symmetry makes frequencies in the same orbit equivalent tests. Thus using the complete list of orbit representatives loses no possible fixed frequency set. This result does not address adaptive tests or tests based on linear combinations of frequencies.

## 4. Three linear conditions and a factored quadratic condition in sixteen variables

**Proposition 3.** If f∈H(16,4) is bent, then c115=0, c57=c113, and c99=Σ_{j∈L}c_j, where L={0,4,8,14,18,22,42,46,52,56,71,81,85,94,98,106}. These three independent linear conditions leave 2¹¹³ coefficient words.

The complete proof is supplied in `T20_DUAS_LEIS_PROVA.md`. Its central step is 64 | A₀ for A_z=Σ_u(−1)^{f(u,u+z)}. Small linear parity identities on the complementary and alternating fibers give 64 | A255 and 32 | A85, while A85=A170. The bent dual's cyclic symmetry gives 64 | A₀+A255−A85−A170. The diagonal fiber is quadratic: its Walsh sum has absolute value 16 when c115=1, and absolute value 32 when c115=0 but c57≠c113. Both cases contradict 64 | A₀.

The parity identities were verified on a complete basis of 116 orbit generators; their verification does not enumerate the 2¹¹⁶ functions. A table reduces them to 25 normalizer classes. The third condition follows from the universal congruence W_f(0)≡A₀+A255 mod128. Its certificate compares the weights modulo four and inner products of the value codes on the 4,080 complete input orbits and the sixteen complementary-fiber orbits. The complete argument and an independent standard-Python checker are supplied in `T20_TRES_LEIS_PROVA.md`.

**Proposition 4.** Under the three linear conditions, W_f(0)≡128Q(c) mod256, where Q is a quadratic Boolean polynomial with five linear terms and 256 quadratic terms. Its quadratic part factors as four products of linear forms with disjoint sets of eight coefficients each. The explicit factors and proof are supplied in `T26_QUARTA_CONDICAO_QUADRATICA.md`.

The proof checks all triple intersections in the value-code basis after substituting c113=c57 and c115=0. The integer XOR expansion then determines the next Walsh bit by a quadratic polynomial. After the third substitution, its graph is the disjoint union of four K₈,₈ components. The coefficient c57 appears only linearly. Toggling c57 and c113 together preserves the three linear laws and reverses Q. Hence Q=0 removes exactly half of the remaining coefficient words. The four necessary conditions leave exactly 2¹¹² words, a fraction 1/16 of H(16,4), and give an explicit parametrization by 112 free bits.

These conditions also have a concrete computational effect. Reproducing the rank21 T16 prefix on CPU recovered 391,804 parents. After the third law, 222,604 remain. The quadratic extension condition is affine in the twelve extension bits for each parent: 173,056 parents admit 2,048 extensions, 25,348 admit 4,096, and 24,200 admit none. The resulting exact T26 domain has 458,244,096 states, compared with the previous 1,604,829,184, before the final energy and Poisson filters. The accompanying Colab package includes CPU/GPU crosschecks and checkpoints; T26 has not yet been executed on a GPU by the assistant.

In the saved T19 sample, the new condition excludes 12,805,538 of the 26,868,006 surviving extensions. This is exact accounting within that sample, not a global T16 exclusion rate. The four conditions do not establish global nonexistence and are not appropriate restrictions when searching for a PC(2) counterexample that need not be bent.

**Proposition 5.** A fifth necessary condition follows from the integer coefficient h_f(0x3f3f) of the sign function. Bentness requires this weight-twelve coefficient to be divisible by 32. Its first nontrivial bit is the sum of products over all 5,775 partitions of that support into three quartets. Cyclic orbit identification reduces this polynomial to 67 terms; under the three T20 laws it factors as c99(c57+ℓ_K)=0, with K explicitly given in `T27_QUINTA_CONDICAO.md`.

After the fourth condition is used to reconstruct c57, the two parities in the fifth condition are jointly uniform in the 112 free parameters. Consequently the five conditions leave exactly 3·2¹¹⁰ coefficient words, a fraction **3/64** of H(16,4). This improves the necessary exclusion to **61/64**. The resulting T27 finite domain, before the final energy and Poisson filters, contains 325,074,944 states. In the saved T19 sample, imposing the fourth and fifth conditions leaves 9,486,004 of the previously surviving 26,868,006 extensions.

The classical 2-adic integer-ANF criterion also yields 389 explicitly constructed first-bit equations at support weights eleven and twelve. After the three linear substitutions, their formal polynomial span has rank 386. This does not establish logical independence or infeasibility. A complete system solver and the stronger higher-bit conditions remain future work.

### A sixth condition with a certified uniform exclusion

The next bit at the same support, h_f(0x3f3f)/16 mod2, must vanish. An affine value code on the 2,016 nonfixed pairs of the half-swap yields cubic formulas on the two branches of the fifth condition. Substituting Q4 through its isolated linear coordinate c23 produces degree-four polynomials on 111 and 110 free coordinates. Their domains have original measures 1/32 and 1/64.

Fixed coordinate restrictions eliminate every higher-degree term involving two selected coordinates. The remaining quadratic forms have polar ranks two and four. Complete enumeration of their affine perturbations certifies minimum weights 1/4 and 3/8. Consequently all six conditions leave at most (3/4)/32+(5/8)/64=17/512 of the original coefficient space, excluding at least 495/512. This is a uniform bound, not the exact density. The certificate checker reconstructs all polynomial coefficients and independently checks 512 original-ANF evaluations. See T28_SEXTA_CONDICAO.md and its standard-Python verifier.

A native-XOR SAT pilot encoding 389 cubic conditions and the additional branch conditions timed out after 45 seconds. No candidates were excluded and no infeasibility certificate was obtained.

### Refining the exclusion through quadratic radicals

On the coordinate restrictions above, derivatives along polar-radical directions are quadratic polynomials in the external coordinates. Unless all such derivatives vanish, the restricted sixth-condition polynomial is balanced. The common-zero densities of these derivatives were evaluated exactly via 64 and four quadratic exponential sums, respectively: 69801930767/4398046511104 and 1/4. Combining these densities with the quadratic Gauss-sum modulus yields an overall pass fraction at most 13538819371023/562949953421312, excluding at least 97.59502256128786% of H(16,4). This refines the same sixth condition; it does not give its exact density. All 68 sums were checked independently by symplectic Gram-Schmidt rather than the pair-elimination algorithm that generated the data. See T29_REFINAMENTO_RADICAIS.md.

### A limitation of the quotient filter

The sixth condition cannot by itself prune any additional T27 quotient state. The original coefficient directions e75 and e42+e81+e94+e106 vanish on all five retained fibers and preserve the first five conditions. Their mixed derivative on each reduced sixth-condition polynomial is identically one. Hence the same quotient state always has lifts with both sixth-bit values. Applying this filter only to a canonical lift would be unsound. This exact obstruction explains why the global exclusion bound does not automatically reduce the GPU domain.

### A seventh, factored quadratic condition

At the periodic support 0x7777, the next integer-ANF bit is R+(1+c99)M+(1+c57)K after the three linear laws. Here K is the parity from the fifth condition, M is the union of the two sides of Q4's third product, and R is an explicit 24-coordinate parity. The complete expansion contains 360 monomials of degree at most two. The free coefficient c5 flips only this condition, preserving the first five. Thus those five together with the seventh leave exactly 3/128 of H(16,4). The two kernel directions from T30 also preserve the seventh, and their constant-one mixed derivative on the sixth condition shows that at most three quarters of the remaining words pass the sixth. All seven conditions therefore leave at most 9/512, excluding at least 503/512. The exact density is open. All coefficients and 256 original-ANF evaluations were verified; see T31_SETIMA_CONDICAO.md.

### Combining the sixth and seventh conditions

Eliminating c5 through the seventh condition and repeating the fixed-quadratic-slice analysis yields a pass fraction at most 107021121/8589934592, excluding at least 98.75411017565057%. Radical derivatives in the zero branch are cubic with all cubic monomials containing c57; conditioning on this bit reduces their exponential sums to quadratic ones. Complete symbolic substitution and independent symplectic checks certify the bound.

Unlike the sixth condition, the seventh descends to the T16 quotient. Its extension gradient is 0x7e. Combining it with the existing Q4 affine extension equation halves the T27 domain to 162,537,472 states: 65,024 parents admit 1,024 extensions and 46,852 admit 2,048. All parent affine-system equivalences and 128 original-ANF evaluations were checked on CPU. The updated T33 A100 package has not been run on GPU.

### A common vector-character bound for nine necessary conditions

The first three linear laws together with Q4, the fifth, and the seventh conditions have a small normal form in eleven aggregate parities. Nine bits are chosen freely, one forbidden two-bit pattern is removed, and two parities are reconstructed. Only 384 of 512 patterns are admissible. The rank of the three laws and eleven aggregate forms is fourteen, so the original fraction is exactly 3/128.

On each of the two resulting affine charts, consider the three necessary integer-ANF bits at supports 0x3f3f, 0x5f5f, 0x6f6f, after the exact substitutions. Their restrictions to S={27,64,73,77,88,92,112} are quadratic with constant polar forms. For any vector P of m such bits, let r_lambda be the polar rank of a nonzero character combination and Z_lambda the common-zero density of its radical derivatives in the external coordinates. Character expansion and the quadratic Gauss-sum modulus give Pr(P=0) ≤ 2^(−m)(1+sum_lambda 2^(−r_lambda/2) Z_lambda). This includes the earlier scalar bounds as m=1.

For m=3, all radical-zero densities can be evaluated exactly after conditioning on at most three coordinates. The two conditional pass bounds are 1375476686851/8796093022208 and 172033/1048576. Their chart measures are 1/64 and 1/128, yielding global pass at most 2097035386883/562949953421312. All coefficients of the six chart models were reconstructed; 192 original-ANF evaluations and 4,800 quadratic sums were checked independently. The exact survivor density is not known. See T34_FORMA_NORMAL.md and T35_NOVE_CONDICOES.md for the consolidated argument.

## 5. Quintic obstruction and open searches

For n=3t+1 and degree at most four, restrict a derivative to a complement of its direction. Its cubic coefficients b_T satisfy

C_f(a)/2^{t+1} ≡ Σ_P ∏_{T∈P}b_T (mod 2),

where P ranges over partitions of the 3t coordinates into triples. The proof follows by the integer expansion of the XOR: only t disjoint cubic monomials contribute to the first nontrivial bit. This is an application of classical monomial-covering divisibility methods [3].

For n=16 and the nine cyclic representatives of directions of weight at most two, this gives nine explicit Boolean polynomials P_d in the 116 coefficients, of degree at most five. PC(2) requires all nine to vanish. The condition is insufficient: an explicit witness has all nine parities zero and nonzero autocorrelations. Nine further witnesses give the nine unit vectors of parity values, establishing linear independence of the nine polynomial functions.

The working conjecture C16 asserts E₂>0 in H(16,4). It remains experimental. A mixed-direction affine search evaluated 268,435,456 coefficient words with possible overlap and found no counterexample; its best E₂ remained 8,192. These evaluations do not exhaust the coefficient space and do not yield a global lower bound.

In twelve variables, an explicit function with coefficient word `0x3573f66ba26` has correlations (0,0,32,0,0,0,0), hence E₂=1,024. This is the smallest positive value allowed by divisibility, but zero has not been excluded. Coordinate and mixed affine searches have not found zero energy. This example motivates a focused attempt to refute a general PC(2) obstruction before proposing an extension of C16 to other dimensions.

Seeds and affine bases are recorded for the nonexhaustive searches. The computational statements are finite and use exact integer arithmetic.
