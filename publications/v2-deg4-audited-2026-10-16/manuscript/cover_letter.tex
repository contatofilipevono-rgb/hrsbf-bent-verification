\noindent Dear Editor,
\medskip

Please consider the manuscript \emph{A reproducible spectral certificate
for the nonexistence of homogeneous quartic rotation symmetric bent
functions in twelve variables}.

The paper presents a computer-assisted exclusion over a binary
coefficient space of dimension 43. It combines fiber Walsh identities,
energy congruences and subspace Poisson constraints with a triangular
enumeration. The necessity of the filters and the coverage of the
enumeration are explained explicitly.

The accompanying reproducibility package contains two implementations
with different spectral evaluation paths, an additional audit of the
finite algebraic foundations, exact execution records, and a Lean file
for selected auxiliary statements. The manuscript clearly distinguishes
these checks from a complete formal proof and does not claim to resolve
the conjecture in arbitrary dimension.

The discussion includes the related 2026 paper of Sun, Shi, Liu and Fu
and avoids unsupported priority claims. I would welcome assessment of
the certificate, the spectral reduction and their relation to existing
results.

Thank you for considering this submission.
