\textbf{Working draft: not submitted. Bibliographic comparison and author declarations remain to be completed.}

The proposed manuscript is entitled \emph{Odd-Order Reduction and a Divisibility Obstruction for Cubic Homogeneous Rotation-Symmetric Bent Functions}.

It presents a computer-assisted nonexistence argument for homogeneous cubic rotation-symmetric bent Boolean functions in positive even dimensions not divisible by 32. The proof combines a quadratic balance lemma for odd-order invariant linear actions, restriction to fixed subspaces, an orbit cancellation argument, and finite exact divisibility checks in sixteen variables. The reduction retains lower-degree terms generated by restriction and excludes the antipodal quadratic orbit.

The standard-library verifier checks 124313 intersection conditions on 42 generators and 12384 additional conditions involving the linear generator. It also checks the smaller base spaces, a nonhomogeneous bent control in twelve variables, and the transfer-matrix example in thirty-two variables. The separate seven-fiber checker reconstructs the original ANFs and verifies a contradiction for one fixed-parameter family in dimension 32; this does not settle the general case.

Cusick--Sanger, Theorem 3.8, covers odd homogeneous degree for dimensions twice an odd prime. Zhang--Gao, Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 3.2, has support-dependent hypotheses. The paper of Sun--Shi--Liu--Fu is confirmed in the publisher's record (volume 94, article 93, 2026; DOI \url{10.1007/s10623-026-01848-4}). Its full theorem statements have not yet been obtained. No claim of originality or strict improvement relative to that paper should be made before this comparison is completed.

The thirty-two-variable example refutes a particular universal compressed-weight divisibility extension; it does not establish a definitive boundary for all divisible-code approaches. No quantum-code application, S-box construction, complete quartic classification in ten variables, or Lean certification is claimed.

The repository is \url{https://github.com/contatofilipevono-rgb/hrsbf-bent-verification}. The recommended command is \texttt{python} \path{avanco_t32/verificador_integrado.py}. Local execution records accompany the revision; timings are machine-dependent and are not represented as Colab execution.
